Money Heist

- SUMMARY -
Money Heist (La Casa de Papel) tells the story of The Professor, a brilliant mastermind who recruits a team of criminals to carry out two major heists — first on the Royal Mint of Spain, where they print billions of euros, and later on the Bank of Spain, where they steal the country’s gold reserves. During the Mint heist, the robbers face emotional struggles, betrayals, and police pressure, but they ultimately escape with the money, though several members die. Years later, they reunite to rescue their captured teammate Rio, leading to a more dangerous mission inside the Bank. The second heist brings new members, losses like Nairobi and Tokyo, and a fierce standoff with the police and military. In the end, The Professor outsmarts the government by secretly swapping the gold and negotiates the gang’s freedom. The robbers are believed dead but actually escape with new identities, becoming symbols of resistance and rebellion.

The Professor (Sergio Marquina) – a genius strategist who plans two major heists: the Royal Mint of Spain and the Bank of Spain.
Berlin – The Professor’s brother and second-in-command, charming yet ruthless.
Tokyo – The impulsive and brave narrator of the series.
Rio – A young, skilled hacker and Tokyo’s love interest.
Nairobi – Expert in printing money and quality control; the team’s emotional core.
Denver – Moscow’s son, known for his unique laugh and kind nature.
Moscow – Denver’s father and tunneling expert.
Helsinki – Serbian soldier and loyal team member.
Oslo – Helsinki’s cousin and fellow soldier.
Palermo – Architect of the Bank of Spain heist and Berlin’s close friend.
Bogotá – Expert welder who helps extract gold.
Marseille – Handles logistics outside the Bank and assists The Professor.
Manila – Denver’s childhood friend who joins the team undercover.
